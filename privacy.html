Thawkeeper: Winter Camp — Privacy Policy

Version: 1.0 · Effective date: 28 September 2026

This policy explains what data the iOS game Thawkeeper: Winter Camp ("the game") and its website collect, why, who receives it, how long it is kept, and what choices and rights you have. A Turkish version is available (Gizlilik Politikası); for users in Türkiye the Turkish version applies, for everyone else this English version applies.

Summary

  • There are no accounts. We never ask for your name, email address, contacts, photos or precise location.
  • Your save file stays on your device and, if iCloud is on, in your own iCloud. We cannot access it.
  • The game uses Google AdMob (ads), GameAnalytics (game analytics) and Sentry (crash reports). These services receive device identifiers, in-game usage and diagnostic data.
  • Your advertising identifier (IDFA) is used only if you allow tracking in the iOS prompt; where required (EEA/UK/Switzerland, Türkiye), personalised ads also need your consent in our consent screens. Refusing changes nothing in the game.
  • We do not sell your data for money. You can manage your choices at any time in the game under Settings > Privacy Choices.

9. Your choices

  • iOS tracking (ATT): On the second in-game day the game shows a short, neutral note and then the iOS prompt asking whether apps may track you. Nothing in the game depends on your answer: no rewards, no locked features, the same number of ads. You can change it anytime in iOS Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking. We never show this prompt if Apple's age signal indicates you are under 18.
  • In-game Privacy Choices: Settings > Privacy Choices opens Google's consent form in the EEA/UK/Switzerland, the "Do Not Sell or Share" choice in U.S. states with privacy laws, and the KVKK consent screen in Türkiye. Changes apply immediately.
  • Stop all collection: Deleting the game stops all collection and removes local data.

10. Deleting your data and other requests

Because there are no accounts, we can find your data at our providers only by your Support ID. You'll find it at the bottom of the game's Settings screen (tap to copy).

  • To delete your data: email seyitkaan5@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion" and your Support ID. We delete the related crash reports at Sentry, ask GameAnalytics to delete the related analytics data, and reply within 30 days. Data held by Google as an independent controller is governed by Google's policy; turning off tracking in iOS Settings stops the IDFA from being shared.
  • Local and iCloud data: deleting the game removes local data. The iCloud copy of your save is in your own Apple Account; you can manage app data in iCloud storage settings (iOS Settings > [your name] > iCloud; exact steps vary by iOS version). We cannot see or delete it for you, but we are happy to help by email.
  • Access, correction, objection: email seyitkaan5@gmail.com with your Support ID; we answer within 30 days, free of charge.

11. Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13. If Apple's age signal (Declared Age Range) indicates the player is under 13: game analytics is turned off, interstitial ads are not shown, rewarded ads are requested as non-personalised with child-appropriate settings, the tracking prompt is never shown, and crash reports continue as for other players (without IP addresses; off in Türkiye). If the signal indicates 13–17, ads are not personalised and the tracking prompt is not shown. Age information stays on the device. Parents can ask us to delete data at seyitkaan5@gmail.com.

12. Your rights in the EEA, UK and Switzerland

This section applies if you use the game in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Switzerland. You have the right to access, rectify and erase your data, to restrict or object to processing (including processing based on legitimate interests), to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time without affecting earlier processing (via Settings > Privacy Choices). Contact us at seyitkaan5@gmail.com (or our representative, if listed in section 1). You may also complain to your local data protection authority (in the UK: the Information Commissioner's Office).

13. U.S. state privacy rights (including California)

  • Categories collected (see section 3): identifiers (install ID, IDFV, IDFA with permission, IP address), internet or other electronic network activity (in-game usage, ad interactions), commercial information (purchase history), approximate geolocation (from IP) and diagnostics. We do not collect sensitive personal information.
  • Sale and sharing: We do not sell personal information for money. Letting our ad partner use identifiers and ad data for personalised (cross-context behavioural) advertising may count as "selling" or "sharing" under some state laws. You can opt out at any time in Settings > Privacy Choices > Do Not Sell or Share My Personal Information, and by not allowing tracking in iOS. We do not sell or share personal information of users we know are under 16.
  • Your rights: depending on your state, you can ask to know, access, correct or delete your personal information and opt out of targeted advertising. Email seyitkaan5@gmail.com with your Support ID. We will not discriminate against you for using these rights. If we decline your request, you can appeal by replying to our answer; if the appeal is denied, you may contact your state Attorney General.

14. Türkiye (KVKK)

If you are in Türkiye, the KVKK Privacy Notice and the Explicit Consent Text (both in Turkish) describe the processing under Law No. 6698 in detail. Game analytics and crash reports are off in Türkiye: no data is sent to GameAnalytics or Sentry from Türkiye. Personalised ads are active only if you give explicit consent on the separate consent screen; otherwise ads are not personalised.

15. Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Crash reports are sent without IP addresses and never include your name, email address, advertising identifier or Game Center ID; they are linked only to a random installation ID. Your save file is protected with an integrity signature. We collect only the data we need and keep it only as long as described above.

16. Changes to this policy

When we change this policy, we update the version and effective date at the top. Material changes are announced in the game with a short notice on the next launch; in Türkiye, the consent screen is shown again if the change affects consent.
