Thawkeeper: Winter Camp

A cozy, one-thumb winter survival game for iPhone.

By day you hunt, fish and haul your sled home before dark. Every purchase visibly changes the world as warmth melts the snow into buildable ground. At night, lantern light keeps the wolves at bay. Grow a tiny campfire into a glowing winter fortress.

  • No energy
  • Rewarded ads always optional
  • No gore
  • iCloud save
  • Game Center
  • English & Turkish
